What we know so far

One Twenty Brickell Residences broke ground as a two-tower development in the heart of Brickell: a 34-story tower of 266 fully furnished condominiums, and a second tower adding 537 multifamily rental apartments to be managed by Sentral.

The Sentral model

Sentral communities offer furnished or unfurnished residences with flexible lease terms — from traditional annual leases to shorter flexible stays — plus hotel-style app-based service. See Sentral Wynwood for the Miami blueprint.

The location

The SW 8th Street corridor in central Brickell: walk to Brickell City Centre, Mary Brickell Village and the Metrorail/Metromover.

The timeline

Construction is underway with the rental tower anticipated in 2027. Pricing, floor plans and the official building name haven't been announced.

Naming note: "Sentral Brickell" is how we (and many renters) refer to the announced Sentral-managed tower at One Twenty Brickell, following Sentral's naming convention (Sentral Wynwood, Sentral East Austin). The official brand name has not yet been announced by the developer and may differ.
