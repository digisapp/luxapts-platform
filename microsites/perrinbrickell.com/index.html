Built to be rented, not converted

The Perrin is ground-up Class A multifamily by Swiss developer Empira Group — designed from the first drawing for renters, with purpose-built amenities and a single professional leasing operation. Not condo inventory rented out one owner at a time. Corwil Architects designed the tower, with interiors by OTJ Architects.

That distinction matters in Brickell, where most of the 2027 delivery wave is for-sale product. Alongside the 310 residences the tower brings roughly 2,500 square feet of ground-floor retail and a 380-space garage to 244 SW 9th Street, two blocks west of the Metrorail.

Questions

Before you ask

When will The Perrin open?

The tower topped out in May 2026 and completion is planned for 2027, with pre-leasing typically beginning a few months before opening. Waitlist members are notified when applications open.

How much will rent be?

Pricing hasn't been announced. New Class A rentals in Brickell currently lease studios from the mid-$2,000s — we'll email the waitlist real numbers when the leasing office publishes them.

What residences will be offered?

310 rental apartments across 26 stories, plus about 2,500 square feet of ground-floor retail and a 380-space garage. The detailed unit mix hasn't been released publicly yet.

Who is developing The Perrin?

Empira Group, a Swiss real estate investment manager. The project was originally announced as Empire Brickell before being rebranded The Perrin. Corwil Architects designed it; Jaxi Builders is the contractor.
