The Residence

Water at your window line

Jade Residences at Brickell Bay stands on the water side of Brickell — no bridge between you and the bay. Floor-to-ceiling glass, open eastern exposures, private elevator arrivals, and layouts that run from one-bedroom pieds-à-terre to full-floor penthouses.
