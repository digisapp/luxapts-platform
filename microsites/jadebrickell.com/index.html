Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent at Jade Brickell?

Current rental listings average about $12,350/month, with smaller residences listing lower and penthouse-level units substantially higher. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll send matching current listings.

Where is Jade Brickell exactly?

1331 Brickell Bay Drive, Miami, FL 33131 — on the Biscayne Bay side of Brickell, a short walk from Brickell City Centre.

Is Jade Brickell the same as Jade Residences?

Yes — the building is formally known as Jade Residences at Brickell Bay; most people call it Jade Brickell or simply Jade.
