Frequently asked questions

When does Namdar Towers start leasing?

The first tower (680 units at 55 NE 2nd Street) received its temporary certificate of occupancy in early 2026, with lease-up underway during the first half of 2026. Join the waitlist to be contacted the moment applications open.

How much will rent be at Namdar Towers?

Official pricing hasn't been published. Comparable new downtown high-rises lease studios from the low-$2,000s — we'll send exact pricing to the waitlist as soon as the leasing office releases it.

What unit types are offered?
