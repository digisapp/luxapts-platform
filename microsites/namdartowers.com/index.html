Questions

Before you ask

When does Namdar Towers start leasing?

Tower One — 680 residences at 55 NE 2nd Street — received its certificate of occupancy in early 2026, with lease-up underway during the first half of 2026. Waitlist members are contacted the moment applications open.

How much will rent be?

Official pricing hasn't been published. Comparable new Downtown towers lease studios from the low $2,000s — exact pricing goes to the waitlist the day the leasing office releases it.

What residences are offered?

Studios, one-bedroom and two-bedroom homes across both towers, with floor-to-ceiling glass throughout.

Who is behind the project?

Namdar Group, which acquired the Downtown site in 2022 and secured $460M in financing to complete both phases.
